How to Use This Calculator

  1. Click the number and operator buttons to build your calculation.
  2. Press the equals button (=) to see the result.
  3. Use C to clear everything, ⌫ to delete the last digit, and % for percentages.
  4. You can also type with your keyboard — numbers and + - * / work.

Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use this calculator?

Click the number and operator buttons to build your calculation, then press the equals button (=) to see the result. You can also use your keyboard.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes. Use the number keys for digits, and + - * / for operations. Press Enter for equals, Backspace to delete, and Escape to clear.

Yes. This calculator is 100% free, runs in your browser, and never requires a sign-up or download.
