How to Use the Wheel Spinner

Type your names or choices into the box (one per line), then click "Spin". The wheel spins and lands on a random entry. Use "Remove Winner" to take the picked entry out and spin again — perfect for picking multiple winners without repeats.

When to Use a Wheel of Names

  • Raffles & giveaways — draw a fair winner.
  • Decisions — let the wheel choose where to eat or what to play.
