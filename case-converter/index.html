What Is a Case Converter?

A case converter changes the capitalization of your text instantly. Whether you pasted a block of ALL-CAPS text that needs fixing, or you need a headline in Title Case, or a variable name in camelCase for your code — this tool does it for free, right in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Supported Text Case Styles

  • UPPERCASE — converts every letter to capital letters.
  • lowercase — converts every letter to small letters.
  • Title Case — capitalizes the first letter of every word.
  • Sentence case — capitalizes the first letter of each sentence.
  • camelCase — joins words and capitalizes each word after the first (for code).
  • PascalCase — joins words and capitalizes every word (for code).
  • snake_case — joins words with underscores, all lowercase.
  • kebab-case — joins words with hyphens, all lowercase.
