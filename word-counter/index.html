Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count words in a text?

Paste or type your text into the box above and the word count updates instantly. You can also see the character count, sentence count, paragraph count and estimated reading time.

What counts as a word?

A word is any group of characters separated by spaces. Numbers, symbols and abbreviations count as words when separated by spaces.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at about 200 words per minute, which is the average adult reading speed.

Is this word counter free?

Yes. This word counter is 100% free, runs in your browser, and never requires a sign-up or download.
