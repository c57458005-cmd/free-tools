Free Online Tools for Everyday Work

FreeTools is a collection of simple, fast and free browser-based tools. Whether you need to convert text case, split names into random groups, spin a wheel of names, generate a random number, or create a strong password — every tool runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is ever uploaded or stored.

All tools are free to use and require no account or installation. Just open the tool you need, do your work, and copy the result.

All Free Tools

  • Calculator — free online calculator for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
  • Case Converter — change text between uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case and kebab-case.
  • Random Group Generator — split a list of names into random, balanced teams or groups for classrooms, workshops and events.
  • Wheel Spinner — spin a wheel of names to pick a random winner, student or decision.
  • Random Number Generator — generate random numbers in any range, with optional unique (no-repeat) mode.
  • Password Generator — create strong, secure random passwords with mixed character types.
  • Word Counter — count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and reading time instantly.
  • Percentage Calculator — calculate X% of Y, what percent one number is of another, and percentage change.
