How to Use the Wheel Spinner

Type your names or choices into the box — separate them with commas (or one per line). Then click "Spin". The wheel spins and lands on a random entry. Use "Remove Winner" to take the picked entry out and spin again — perfect for picking multiple winners without repeats.

When to Use a Wheel of Names

  • Classrooms — randomly pick a student to answer a question.
  • Raffles & giveaways — draw a fair winner.
  • Decisions — let the wheel choose where to eat or what to play.
  • Icebreakers — spin to pick a topic or activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I spin a wheel of names?

Type your names or options into the box, separated by commas or new lines, then click "Spin". The wheel rotates and stops on a random entry. You can remove a winner and spin again.

Is this wheel spinner free?

Yes. This wheel spinner is 100% free, runs in your browser, and never requires a sign-up or download.

Does this wheel spinner work on mobile?

Yes. The wheel spinner is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops alike.

Are my names kept private?

Yes. The wheel runs locally in your browser. Your names never leave your device and are never stored or uploaded.
